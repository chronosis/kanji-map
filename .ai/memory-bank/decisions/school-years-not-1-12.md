# Decision: School years are 小1–小6, not 1–12

MEXT’s 学年別漢字配当表 assigns kanji only to **elementary** years 1–6.
The remaining 常用漢字 are taught in junior high (roughly school years 7–9) **without**
a national year-by-year table; high school (10–12) has no joyo year list.

UI labels: `小1`…`小6` and `中学+` for post-elementary jōyō. Do not invent grades 7–12.
